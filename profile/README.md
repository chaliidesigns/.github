## About

Chalii is Arusha street slang for the one you call when something needs sorting out. It is also how we work.

Chalii Designs is a design agency founded in Arusha. We work remotely and in hybrid teams across Tanzania, from Dar es Salaam to Morogoro, Kilimanjaro, Tanga and beyond.

## What we do

| Service                   | What it covers                                                            |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Digital product design    | Apps and digital products, designed end to end                            |
| Web design                | Websites that are clear, fast and on brand                                |
| UX research and UI design | Research that shapes the product, and interfaces that make it easy to use |
| Brand identity (extra)    | Logos, visual identity and guidelines                                     |
| Web development (extra)   | The websites we design, built by the same team                            |
